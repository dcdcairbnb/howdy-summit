Thank you for the questions. Happy to clarify the business model.

**Howdy Summit County is completely free. There are no accounts, no fees, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, and no paid tiers.** Every feature in the app is available immediately on launch without signing in or paying anything. There is no premium content, no locked functionality, and nothing a user can buy from us.

The app is a free visitor guide to Summit County, Colorado. It is funded entirely by affiliate commissions from third-party travel partners, which we disclose in the app.

Answers to your specific questions:

**1. Who are the users that will use the paid services in the app?**

There are no paid services in the app. All users have full access to every feature for free.

Some links in the app point to third-party websites where a user may choose to book real-world travel services, for example a hotel room or a guided snowmobile tour. Those transactions happen entirely on the third party's own website or app, between the user and that company. Howdy Summit is not a party to them, does not process them, and does not receive payment from the user.

**2. Where can users purchase the services that can be accessed in the app?**

Nothing is purchased inside the app, and nothing purchased elsewhere unlocks anything inside the app.

Where the app links out, it links to established third-party travel sites: Expedia, Booking.com, Viator, GetYourGuide and Amazon. These open in the device browser. We earn an affiliate commission if a user books through one of those links, at no additional cost to the user. This is disclosed in the app footer and in our Disclaimer and Privacy page.

**3. What specific types of previously purchased services can a user access in the app?**

None. There is no account system, so the app has no concept of a returning or paying customer, and there is nothing to restore. A user who has booked a hotel through an affiliate link gets no different experience in the app than a user who has not.

**4. What paid content, subscriptions, or features are unlocked within the app that do not use In-App Purchase?**

None. No content, feature or capability in the app is gated behind a payment of any kind, whether made inside or outside the app.

Everything the affiliate links point to is a **physical good or real-world service** consumed outside the app: hotel and lodging stays, guided tours and activities, ski and gear rentals, ground transport, and physical retail goods. Per Guideline 3.1.3(e) and 3.1.5(a), physical goods and services consumed outside the app do not use In-App Purchase.

We suspect the **Split Costs** feature may have prompted this question, so to be explicit about it: Split Costs is a free calculator for a group of friends splitting shared trip expenses, for example a restaurant bill. A user may optionally enter their own Venmo, Cash App or PayPal handle so the app can generate a standard deep link that opens that third-party app with the amount pre-filled. Howdy Summit does not process, hold, transfer or take a fee from any of that money. It is an ordinary person-to-person payment between friends, made in a separate app, and it is entirely optional. No part of the app is unlocked by it.

**5. How do users obtain an account? Do users have to pay a fee to create an account?**

There are no accounts. There is no sign-up, no login, and no fee. The app opens straight into full functionality.

Users may optionally provide an email address to receive a free trip guide. That is a plain email subscription with a one-click unsubscribe, costs nothing, and grants no additional access in the app. It is not an account and is not required to use any feature.

To summarise: the app is free, has no accounts and no in-app purchases, unlocks nothing behind a paywall, and earns revenue solely through affiliate commissions on physical goods and real-world services booked on third-party websites.

Happy to provide any further detail you need.
