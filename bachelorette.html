Before You Arrive

  • Pedal TavernBook 4+ weeks out. Weekends sell out fast.
  • Group DinnerHusk, Etch, or Yolan need 3+ weeks notice for 6+ people.
  • Matching GearOrder tank tops, sashes, and props online. Cheaper than buying on Broadway.
  • BNA TransfersPre-book Welcome Pickups or Kiwitaxi for groups of 6+.
  • Pickup SpotSave your rental address in Uber and Lyft for the whole group.
Day 1

Arrive and Lower Broadway

Afternoon: Settle In

Drop bags. Grab coffee at Frothy Monkey or Crema.

Take a group photo at the I Believe in Nashville mural in 12 South before it gets busy.

Late Afternoon: Honky Tonks

Tootsie's, Robert's Western World, Acme Feed and Seed, Honky Tonk Central.

All have free live music starting around 10am. Tip the bands.

Dinner

Hot chicken at Hattie B's. Ole Red for a country menu and rooftop. The Stillery for late-night.

Evening: Rooftop

L.A. Jackson (12 South), White Limozeen (Graduate Hotel), Bobby Hotel rooftop, or Fairlane rooftop.

Recovery Setup

Drink water before bed. Book mobile IV recovery for 9am tomorrow.

Day 2

Brunch, Photos, Activity

Brunch

Biscuit Love (no reservations, arrive before 9am for the bonut).

Adele's for upscale. The Hampton Social for rosé all day.

Photo Run

I Believe in Nashville (12 South). What Lifts You wings (The Gulch). Make Music Not War mural (12 South). Patsy Cline mural (Berry Hill).

Afternoon Activity

Paint and sip class. Mechanical bull at Whiskey Row. Distillery tour at Nelson's Green Brier or Corsair.

Big group: book a pedal tavern or party bus through Lower Broadway.

Dinner

The Gulch: Bartaco, Bakersfield, or Adele's.

12 South: Edley's BBQ, Burger Up, or Urban Grub.

Evening

Bluebird Cafe songwriter round (book ahead). Then back to Broadway for one more honky tonk.

Day 3

Brunch, Day Trip, Goodbye

Brunch

Pancake Pantry (Hillsboro Village). Marche (East Nashville). Frothy Monkey for groups.

Day Trip Options

Leiper's Fork distillery and village (40 min). Franklin Main Street (30 min). Cheekwood Estate gardens (20 min).

Last Meal

Hot chicken at Prince's, Bolton's, or Hattie B's. Loveless Cafe for biscuits if you have a car.

Souvenirs

Hatch Show Print posters. Goo Goo Cluster shop on Lower Broadway. Third Man Records (Jack White's label).

Logistics

Group Transportation

6+ People

Party bus or limo. Search Viator or GetYourGuide for local providers.

2 to 5 People

Uber XL is cheapest. Set the pickup point at your rental in advance.

BNA Airport

Pre-book Welcome Pickups (English-speaking driver) or Kiwitaxi (fixed price). No surge pricing.

Recovery

Recovery Tools

Mobile IV

Reset IV, Drip Hydration, NV Drips. They come to your rental at 9am to noon. Group rates at 4+.

Local Hangover Ritual

Pancake Pantry biscuits and Frothy Monkey coffee. The Diner stays open late.

Stay Hydrated

Nashville heat plus rooftop drinking dehydrates faster than you think. Water between drinks.
