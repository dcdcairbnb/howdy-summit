Get instant picks for hot chicken, honky tonks, live music, and rooftop bars with one-tap rideshare, real ticket links, live weather, and group location sharing. No signup, no paywall.

Long description (130 words)

Long Howdy Nash is a free Nashville travel guide and AI concierge built for visitors who want the real Music City. Ask anything in plain English: "best hot chicken near me," "live music tonight under twenty bucks," "most photogenic rooftop bar." Howdy Nash answers like a local with up-to-the-minute data from Ticketmaster, SeatGeek, Foursquare, Google Places, and the National Weather Service. Built-in features include bachelorette weekend planning with mobile IV, pedal taverns, and group location sharing for crews up to 20 people. One-tap rideshare to any spot. Real ticket links. Restaurant deep links to Resy, OpenTable, and Yelp. Free forever. No signup. No paywall. No ads. Available at howdynash.com and on the iOS App Store.

Founder bio

Bio Dan Crose is the founder of Howdy Nash. He built the app after watching too many friends and family roll into town for the first time and miss the spots locals love. The app started as a personal cheat sheet for his Airbnb guests and turned into a free public guide. Reach Dan at howdy@howdynash.com.
