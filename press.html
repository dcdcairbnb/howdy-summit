Standard description (50 words)

Medium Howdy Summit is a free Summit County, Colorado travel concierge built for ski trips, summer road trips, and first-time visitors. Ask anything in plain English. Get instant picks for lift tickets, breweries, apres-ski, trailheads, and dinner across Breckenridge, Frisco, Dillon, Silverthorne, Keystone, and Copper Mountain. No signup, no paywall.

Long description (130 words)

Long Howdy Summit is a free Summit County, Colorado travel guide and AI concierge built for visitors who want the high country the way locals ski it. Ask anything in plain English: "best apres-ski near the Breck gondola," "easy hike with a lake view," "where's the wait shortest for dinner in Frisco tonight." Howdy Summit answers like a local with up-to-the-minute data from Google Places, Ticketmaster, SeatGeek, Foursquare, and the National Weather Service. Built-in features cover all six towns, the ski resorts, restaurants and breweries, hiking and biking trails, festivals, and family activities, plus group location sharing so your crew can find each other on the mountain. One-tap rideshare. Real ticket links. Restaurant booking deep links. Free forever. No signup. No paywall. No ads. Available at howdysummitcounty.com.

Founder bio

Bio Dan Crose is the founder of Howdy Summit. He built the app after watching too many friends and family drive up I-70 for the first time and miss the spots locals love. The app started as a personal cheat sheet for his Airbnb guests and turned into a free public guide to all six towns in Summit County. Reach Dan at howdy@howdysummitcounty.com.

Key features for coverage

  • AI concierge: Plain-English questions, local-quality answers
  • Six towns: Breckenridge, Frisco, Dillon, Silverthorne, Keystone, Copper Mountain
  • Ski resorts: Breckenridge, Keystone, Copper, Arapahoe Basin, Loveland
  • Apres-ski and breweries: Where to land after the last chair
  • Trails: Hiking and mountain biking, from lake loops to alpine passes
  • Live event data: Concerts and festivals with real ticket prices and links
  • Group location sharing: 6, 12, or 24-hour share with auto-delete
  • One-tap rideshare: Uber and Lyft deep links to any spot
  • Free transit: Summit Stage and Free Ride shuttle info
  • One-tap delivery: DoorDash, Uber Eats links
